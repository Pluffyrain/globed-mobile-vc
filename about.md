# Globed Mobile Voice Chat

A button for Globed's voice chat, for mobilen players

## Requirements
Talking (sending your voice) only works with a patched build of
Globed that has mobile voice sending enabled, on official Globed the button will still appear, but holding it wont sendt
anything. Make sure whoever you share this with also has the
patched Globed file

## How to use
- The button appears bottom left automatically whenever youre connected
  to a Globed session
- Hold it to talk (green), release tostop (red)
- To reposition it, turn on move Button in this mods settings, drag it
  where you want, then turn the setting back off.
- Turn off Enable Button in settings to hide the button
